HOW TO PLAY

🔱 OUTRUN LORD SHIVA:

Lord Shiva is chasing you! If you stumble on obstacles, Shiva closes in. Dodge cleanly to maintain distance!

🛺 ONCOMING VEHICLES:

Watch out for oncoming Auto-Rickshaws & street vans driving towards you! Listen for their horns and steer out of the lane.

📱 CONTROLS:
  • TILT PHONE / A, D: Steer left / right
  • SWIPE UP / W, SPACE: Jump
  • SWIPE DOWN / S: Slide
